02 Channel Distribution Plan — Hub & Spoke

The blog post is the hub. Every channel drives the right executive into it through the right format at the right moment. One thesis. Five touchpoints. Six weeks. By the time a target executive is on a sales call, the narrative is already familiar.

Organic / Founder Voice

LinkedIn Founder Post

One idea from the article, written in the Founder’s own voice. Not a summary — a single provocation. Ends on unresolved tension. The article is the resolution.

Executives trust other executives. Lateral authority transfer. No pitch — pure provocation that opens pattern-matching in the reader’s own operational memory.
Organic / Owned Audience

Newsletter — Provenance Framework

Seven days after the LinkedIn post. Delivers the three-question Operational Provenance Framework as a standalone, immediately usable tool. Both paths lead back to the hub.

Pre-sales qualification embedded inside content. No friction, no form. Converts passive readers into active framework users.
Organic / Long-form

Blog + GEO Positioning

Published ungated. Structured to answer the exact queries enterprise buyers run before evaluating an AI vendor. Proof anchors with specific metrics serve GEO positioning — making the article citable by AI-powered research tools.

Ungated friction removal + AI search citation loops create compounding inbound without ad spend.
Paid / ABM Precision

LinkedIn ABM Paid Social

Same thesis, vertical-specific vocabulary. An airline COO sees crew coordination failure copy. A bank CFO sees compliance audit exposure copy. Seven-word headline maximum. One proof anchor. One link.

Runs concurrent with the organic post. Executives who see both within 48 hours experience reinforcement, not repetition.

Surgical vocabulary match triggers category recognition before the first sales call.
Paid / Brand Priming

Digital OOH — Airport & Enterprise Corridors

Seven words or fewer. O’Hare, JFK, Canary Wharf. No product pitch. A single question that sticks. Function is not conversion — it is priming. Activated during the two weeks surrounding major industry conferences.

When the ABM ad finds the executive two days later, the brand is already recognized. Recognition precedes persuasion by design.

04–B Visual Direction

ABM Digital Ad

ConceptOperations control center. Lone operator facing live monitoring screens — shot from behind. No faces. Real data visible: timestamps, route IDs, exception flags. The human is present but secondary to the system.
PaletteDesaturated 40%. Cool blue-green from screens. No warm tones. Functional, not aspirational.
TypeInter Black 900 headline on translucent white bar. Inter Light 300 body. Extreme weight contrast — the shift is the design system. No stylization.
SignalThe COO recognizes the scene before reading a word. Recognition precedes persuasion. The ad confirms what they already know about operational fragility.

OOH Airport Billboard

ConceptPure typographic execution. No photography. No graphic elements. Off-white background. Black type only. In an airport full of competing visual noise, absence of noise signals confidence.
PaletteHappyRobot brand base. Nothing else.
TypeInter Black 900 at maximum optical scale. Attribution in mono Light 300. Two cognitive registers — impact, then attribution. Weight contrast is the only graphic element.
SignalThe brand that doesn’t fight for attention, in a context where every other brand does. Restraint at billboard scale reads as established authority.
