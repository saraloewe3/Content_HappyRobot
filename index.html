02 Channel Distribution

Hub and Spoke.

The blog post is the hub. Every channel drives the right executive into it through the right format at the right moment. One thesis. Five touchpoints. Six weeks.

A master narrative is meaningless without structural distribution. We weaponize LinkedIn as the organic and paid ABM core to intercept enterprise decision-makers at the exact moment they are evaluating operational risk. Simultaneously, Out-Of-Home assets deployed inside Tier-1 airport terminals, Dallas, Miami, and JFK, capture aviation and logistics executives when operational delays are physically occurring around them. Physical placement becomes a high-intent conversion trigger. The result is a six-week compounding flywheel: organic trust, paid precision, and environmental priming converge on a single ungated asset, the blog post, which does the closing work.
